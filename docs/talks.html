Conference talks, posters, seminar talks, and workshops by He Wang.

Talks, Posters, and Seminar Presentations

This page collects selected conference talks, posters, seminar talks, and workshops. The talks are centered around algebraic topology, group theory, Lie algebras associated to groups, formality properties, resonance varieties, Chen ranks, configuration spaces, and related topics.

Featured Topic Areas

Formality Properties

Talks on 1-formality, graded-formality, filtered-formality, rational homotopy theory, Malcev Lie algebras, and their applications to finitely generated groups.

Resonance and Chen Ranks

Presentations on resonance varieties, Chen ranks, Alexander invariants, cohomology jumping loci, and resonance–Chen rank formulas.

Braid-Like Groups

Talks involving pure braid groups, welded braid groups, McCool groups, configuration spaces, and algebraic invariants of braid-type groups.
